Splits a JML clause whose condition is a conjunction, such as ensures a && b;, into one clause per part, which means the same.

When OpenJML cannot prove a clause, Verify with OpenJML then says which part it cannot prove. It works for requires, ensures, invariants, loop invariants, assert, assume and check, when the parts are joined by && only: a condition such as a || b && c or a && b ==> c means something else in parts.
